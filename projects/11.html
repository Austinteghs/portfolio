Global Billionaires Dashboard (Excel)

Data Analysis · Excel · Data Visualization · Wealth Analytics

ExcelPivot TablePivot ChartData CleaningData VisualizationWealth Analysis

Project Overview

An Excel-based analytics dashboard profiling the world's wealthiest individuals — exploring net worth distribution, industry concentration, geographic spread, and demographic patterns among global billionaires.

Technical Build

  • Data Cleaning: Standardized country names, corrected net worth formatting, handled missing age and industry values, removed duplicate entries
  • Pivot Tables: Aggregated billionaire counts and total net worth by country, industry, age bracket, and gender
  • Pivot Charts: Bar charts for top countries by billionaire count, pie charts for industry share, scatter plot of age vs net worth
  • Dashboard: Single-sheet interactive dashboard with slicers for region and industry filtering

Key Insights

  • The U.S. and China account for over 50% of global billionaires combined
  • Technology and Finance are the dominant wealth-generating industries
  • The average age of billionaires is 65, but self-made tech billionaires skew significantly younger
  • Less than 12% of global billionaires are women
