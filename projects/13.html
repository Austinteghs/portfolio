Project Overview

A 24-hour PostgreSQL training series delivered at POShem Technologies (November – December 2024), covering database fundamentals through to advanced querying and BI integration.

Curriculum Breakdown

  • DDL (Data Definition Language): Creating and managing databases, tables, schemas, indexes, and constraints
  • DML (Data Manipulation Language): INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE, and complex SELECT queries including JOINs, subqueries, CTEs, and window functions
  • TCL (Transaction Control Language): COMMIT, ROLLBACK, SAVEPOINT — ensuring data integrity in multi-step operations
  • pgAdmin: Hands-on use of the pgAdmin GUI for database management and query execution
  • Power BI Integration: Connecting PostgreSQL as a live data source for dashboard development

Format

Role: Instructor at POShem Technologies | Duration: November – December 2024 | Total Hours: 24 hours of live instruction across multiple sessions, blending theory with hands-on exercises.

Impact

Students progressed from zero SQL knowledge to writing multi-table analytical queries and connecting their databases to Power BI for visualization — in just 24 hours of instruction.
