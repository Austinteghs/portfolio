HIV Data Analysis — Abia & Taraba States, Nigeria (2019–2021)

Data Analysis · Public Health · Excel · Data Visualization

ExcelPivot TableData VisualizationData CleaningPublic HealthNigeriaHIV

Project Overview

A comprehensive analysis of HIV data for Abia and Taraba states in Nigeria (2019–2021) — undertaken during my Graduate Internship at the National Agency for the Control of AIDS (NACA). The project covers data cleaning, trend analysis, and visualization with a structured report.

Context

This work was part of NACA's data-driven approach to tracking HIV program performance. Clean, accurate data is critical for allocating treatment resources, measuring ART coverage, and reporting to international health bodies.

Process

  • Data Cleaning: Resolved inconsistent facility name spellings, removed duplicate patient records, standardized date formats across the two-state dataset
  • Analysis: Compared HIV new case rates, ART enrollment, and testing volumes across Abia and Taraba, broken down by year, LGA, gender, and age group
  • Visualization: Excel pivot charts and conditional formatting dashboards showing trend lines, facility-level performance, and demographic breakdowns

Deliverables

A cleaned dataset, an Excel dashboard with interactive pivot table slicers, and a comprehensive written report documenting the methodology, findings, and programmatic recommendations — formatted for a public health audience.

Impact

Findings fed into NACA's state-level program review, supporting resource allocation decisions and gap identification in HIV testing and treatment coverage.
