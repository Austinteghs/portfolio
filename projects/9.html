Teghs Company Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

Business Intelligence · Power BI · DAX · Data Modeling

Power BIDAXPower QueryData AnalysisData ModellingSales Analytics

Project Overview

An interactive Power BI sales dashboard for Teghs Company — tracking revenue performance, product category trends, regional sales distribution, and sales rep productivity.

Technical Build

  • Data Model: Star schema with a central sales fact table linked to date, product, customer, and region dimension tables
  • Power Query: Data cleaning and transformation — merged datasets from multiple sources, handled date discrepancies, created calculated columns
  • DAX Measures: YTD Revenue, MoM Growth %, Running Total, Top N Products filter, and custom KPI calculations
  • Visuals: Revenue trend (line chart), regional heatmap, product performance matrix, and KPI cards with conditional formatting

Key Business Questions Answered

  • Which regions are over- or under-performing against target?
  • What is the month-over-month revenue trend by product category?
  • Which sales reps are driving the most revenue this quarter?

Skills Demonstrated

End-to-end BI development: data modeling, ETL in Power Query, DAX authoring, and dashboard UX design for executive audiences.
